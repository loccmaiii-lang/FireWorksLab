Intended direction: A quiet, dark, instrument-grade production tool in the Unreal / Houdini / DaVinci family: a near-black viewport as the stage, blue-grey panels pushed back, one mint accent reserved for "live / current / go" (UE connected dot, edited-layer outline, tab underline, playhead, Export), 28 px row rhythm, hairline module boundaries, and craft details such as viewfinder crop marks around the bake frame and a filmstrip of baked frames above the export range. The aim is calm density: everything a technical artist needs on one screen, nothing decorative, with the firework as the only warm, luminous thing on the screen.

Contrast with Pentagram / Apple / Teenage Engineering: Pentagram would build this from one typographic system first: two sizes, two weights, tabular numerals, and a single right-hand value edge shared by inspector values, section summaries and the transport readout. Here bold carries eight different meanings, and numbers end on four slightly different edges. Apple (Final Cut / Motion / Reality Composer) would split toolbar scope without compromise. The window toolbar holds document actions. The inspector holds only the selection's properties, on a strict label | full-width control | value grid, with row actions appearing on hover. The timeline height would follow its content, and the viewport and timeline would get the space the inspector does not need. Accent would be spent only on the one thing that is live, the playhead and the bake progress, and not on a solid mint Export slab that outshines the firework all day.
