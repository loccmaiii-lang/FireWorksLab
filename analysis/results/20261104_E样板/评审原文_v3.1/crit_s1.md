Intended direction: A dark, low-chroma "pro DCC instrument" in the lineage of Unreal, Houdini and Resolve. The content leads (the burst sits alone in a black viewport). The layout is a disciplined three-column frame plus a timeline. Mint is reserved as the one "live" signal: connection dots, the current tab, the edit outline, the playhead and the single filled Export button. The geometry is flat and square-cornered, and the type is small and dense for all-day use. The aim is calm, technical, quiet chrome that gets out of the way of the fire. In broad strokes it gets there: palette discipline is good, nothing shouts, and the burst is clearly the hero.

Contrast with Pentagram / Apple / Teenage Engineering: Pentagram would start from a typographic system, not a component kit. There would be two or three sizes with real contrast, and numerals (timecode, values, frame counts) would be treated as the primary typography. One vertical grid would be shared by the tree, the timeline label column and the inspector label column, so the three panes lock together. Here everything sits at roughly the same 13 px regular in two greys, and each pane has its own gutters. Apple (Final Cut / Motion / Reality Composer) would give every toolbar one grammar. Navigation would be a segmented control with an obvious selected state, tools would be icons from one symbol family, and commands would be words, each in its own cluster. Inspector rows would come from one template, and toggles would read instantly as on or off. This screen mixes icon-only, text-only, icon+text, boxed segments and dropdowns in the same strip, and the two page entries look exactly like status text. Teenage Engineering would make the time and bake instruments the characterful centrepiece: a confident mono numeral readout, lit/unlit indicators with one strict grammar, and controls with a recognisable physical identity. Here the transport is a row of generic glyphs, the bake strip is a row of growing dots that reads like a loading animation, and the toggles look like placeholder squares. All three studios would keep this palette and these square corners. What they would add is hierarchy, a grid and one consistent control language.

Biggest gaps:
1. Flat typographic hierarchy. The product name, page entries, tree items, library names, inspector labels, toolbar commands and status text are all near the same size and weight, separated only by two greys. Only the timecode "2.00 / 5.24 s" and the bold section heads break the monotony. An all-day user has to read where they should be able to scan. The numerals (values, timecode, bake 50/75, frame range) are never treated as the instrument they are.
2. The toolbars have no consistent grammar. Viewport bar: two boxed segment groups, then five unlabelled stock icons (#, pencil, expand, two panel-layout glyphs), then the text button "预览设置". Inspector bar: undo/redo icons, a disabled text "还原", an unexplained duplicate-squares icon, text "保存 / 另存", an amber "⚠ 1 层", and a split Export button. Transport: icons, a "1× ⌄" dropdown, the highlighted loop button, a stopwatch glyph, text "选项 ⌄", and icon+text "缩略图". Nothing in these strips tells you which items are view state, which are commands and which are navigation.
3. The top bar is a flat string of items with no structure. The three statuses, "设置", the two page entries and the GPU name are all the same weight. "设置" is wedged between the status group and the page entries. The gaps are irregular (about 15–40 px). The page entries 资源交付 / 资源编排 have no selected state and read as two more labels. The 4 px status dots are too small to work as at-a-glance indicators. The outlined digit "keycap" badge means three different things: unread updates beside the version, a count beside 资源交付, and the "?" shortcut key in the status bar.
4. Library status has no hierarchy of urgency. 制作中 / 待验收 / 新 / 已通过 are all small grey words right-aligned on line two, and 待验收 is only marginally brighter. For this user, "awaiting review" is the actionable state, and it should be the one thing that pops while 已通过 recedes. The thumbnails are also exposed unevenly: 千轮 and 柳 are nearly black squares, and the comet/tail effects shrink to 3 px slivers in a 40 px square. They need normalised exposure and framing per pattern type.
5. Inspector rows break their own template:
   - On/off toggles differ only in luminance (dark box with grey square vs. grey box with white square) and do not read as switches.
   - The disabled 湍流尺度 slider is shortened so its hint "强度为 0 时无效" can intrude into the value column.
   - A cryptic "A." floats after 曝光.
   - The section-level and row-level reset icons are the same glyph in the same column.
   - The section chevrons are about 6 px and hang outside the left text edge, so they are almost invisible.
   - Below 输出, about 260 px of the column is dead. The pane looks unfinished rather than intentionally open.
6. The timeline duplicates the tree but draws it in a different language. The tree uses glyphs for 星 / 火花 / 余烬, and the timeline repeats the same 11 rows as plain text. The selected layer's near-white bar and white diamonds are the brightest marks in the lower half and compete with the mint playhead. The "烘焙" filmstrip sits above the ruler as a row of growing circles that looks like a progress spinner. The range readout "0.35 – 2.85 s" and the in/out/reset buttons are jammed into the ruler's label gutter.
7. Alignment and grid drift:
   - The timeline label column (about 195 px) matches neither the left column (300 px) nor the inspector's label column.
   - In the viewport, the overlay baselines disagree: "PC 30 fps tick 60" sits about 9 px lower than "距离 1000 m 预览曝光 1.00 倍".
   - The tree has no header row, so its root item rides in the toolbar band and acts as a heading by accident.
   - Panel gutters vary between 12, 13 and 16 px from pane to pane.

- Segmented controls everywhere in one identical boxed style: render mode, zoom, category filter, 单次/循环 and the loop button. They all look alike whether they are page mode, view option, filter or parameter.

- Right-aligned status words on list rows (a generic list-template pattern).
- The stray "A." glyph after 曝光, which reads like placeholder or truncated text.

Remove the inline disabled hint (dim the whole row and put the reason in the tooltip or a sub-line). Remove the cryptic "A.". Make toggles read as switches with an unmistakable on state.
